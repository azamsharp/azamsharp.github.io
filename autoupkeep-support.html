Frequently asked questions

How do I add a vehicle?

Open the Vehicles area and use the add option. Enter your vehicle's details and current mileage, then save it.

How do I schedule maintenance?

Go to Maintenance and choose the add option. Select a maintenance template or create a custom task, choose a vehicle, and set the due date, mileage, or recurring schedule.

Why didn't I receive a reminder?

Check that notifications are allowed for AutoUpkeep in iOS Settings and that your task has a reminder configured. For mileage-based tasks, keep the vehicle's odometer reading up to date; the app cannot know miles driven unless that information is updated.

How can I record a completed service?

Open the relevant maintenance task and mark it complete. Where available, add the service date, mileage, cost, and notes to keep a useful history.

Can I export my maintenance history?

AutoUpkeep supports PDF maintenance reports. Look for the report or export option in the app to generate a shareable record of your vehicle's maintenance.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions purchased through Apple are managed in your Apple Account. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and select AutoUpkeep. You can also visit Apple's billing support.

How do I request a refund?

For App Store purchases, refund requests are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email azamsharp@gmail.com with a short description. For bugs, include your device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots are welcome if they don't expose sensitive information.

A quick reminder

AutoUpkeep helps you organize service schedules and records, but it does not replace your vehicle manufacturer's maintenance guidance or advice from a qualified mechanic. Follow the recommendations for your specific vehicle.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a maintenance task?

Open HomeUpkeep and add a new maintenance task. Enter the task details, choose a schedule, and configure reminders as needed.

Does HomeUpkeep include suggested tasks?

Yes. HomeUpkeep includes a catalog of more than 70 suggested home maintenance tasks. You can browse the catalog and add tasks relevant to your home.

How do reminders work?

HomeUpkeep can notify you when maintenance tasks are due. Make sure notifications are enabled for HomeUpkeep in your device's Settings.

Can I track appliances and equipment?

Yes. You can keep equipment details such as brand, model number, installation date, warranty information, photos, and related maintenance tasks.

Does HomeUpkeep support iCloud syncing?

HomeUpkeep supports syncing compatible app data through iCloud. Make sure you're signed in to iCloud on your devices and that iCloud access is enabled for the app.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Open your device's Settings, tap your Apple Account, and select Subscriptions. Choose HomeUpkeep to manage or cancel your subscription.

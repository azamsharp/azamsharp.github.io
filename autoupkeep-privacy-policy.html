Privacy Policy

How AutoUpkeep handles information when you manage your vehicles, schedule maintenance, and track service records.

1. Overview

AutoUpkeep is a vehicle maintenance app that helps you organize vehicle details, schedule service reminders, record completed maintenance, review expenses, and export maintenance reports. This policy explains the types of information involved in these features and the choices available to you.

AutoUpkeep is not intended to collect sensitive personal information. Please avoid entering sensitive information in free-text notes or uploaded images unless necessary.

2. Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, you may enter or add:

  • Vehicle information, such as make, model, year, nickname, odometer readings, and vehicle photos.
  • Maintenance information, including service types, due dates, mileage intervals, completion dates, notes, and expenses.
  • Documents or images you choose to attach to vehicle or service records, if supported in your app version.
  • Preferences such as distance units and reminder settings.

The content of notes, images, and exported reports is determined by you.

3. How information is used

Information you enter is used to provide the features you request: displaying vehicle profiles, organizing maintenance schedules, calculating upcoming or overdue tasks from the dates and mileage you provide, recording service history and costs, and generating reports.

AutoUpkeep does not require access to your contacts, microphone, or precise location for its core maintenance features.

4. Storage and syncing

Your vehicle and maintenance information is stored using the storage mechanisms provided by the app and your device. If a version of AutoUpkeep offers cloud synchronization or device backup, the applicable Apple services and your device or account settings may also affect where copies are retained.

Important: Before publishing this policy, confirm and specify whether your production app stores records only on-device, uses iCloud/CloudKit, or sends records to a developer-operated server. Replace this paragraph with the precise behavior. Do not publish an ambiguous storage disclosure.

5. Notifications

With your permission, AutoUpkeep may send maintenance reminders and other notifications related to schedules you create. You can change notification permissions at any time in your device's Settings. Notification scheduling may use maintenance dates and mileage values you provide.

6. Premium subscriptions

AutoUpkeep may offer optional paid subscriptions. Purchases, renewals, and subscription management are handled through Apple's App Store and associated payment services. AutoUpkeep does not receive your full payment card number from Apple. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account's subscription settings.

Apple's handling of purchase and payment information is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

7. PDF reports and sharing

When you choose to generate or share a maintenance report, it may contain vehicle details, service history, mileage, notes, and expenses from your records. You control whether to save or share the exported PDF. Once you share a report with another person or service, their handling of it is outside AutoUpkeep's control.

8. Third-party services

AutoUpkeep uses Apple platform services where needed for app distribution, subscription purchases, notifications, and operating-system functionality. If the app includes additional analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or other third-party SDKs, those services and their data practices must be disclosed here before release.

9. Data retention and deletion

You can edit or remove vehicle and maintenance records using the app's available controls. Deleting the app generally removes its locally stored app data from that device, but copies in device backups, synced storage, or other services may remain under their separate retention rules. Canceling a subscription does not necessarily delete app data.

If your version of AutoUpkeep supports cloud accounts or server-side records, provide a corresponding account or data deletion process here.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to support the security of app information, including relying on security protections provided by the operating system and applicable platform services. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

11. Children's privacy

AutoUpkeep is intended for general vehicle owners and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a service we operate, please contact us.

12. Your choices and privacy rights

You can choose what vehicle and maintenance information to enter, change notification permissions, and manage your App Store subscriptions. Depending on your location and how the app processes information, you may have additional rights concerning access, correction, or deletion. Contact us to submit a privacy request.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in AutoUpkeep, its features, or legal requirements. When we do, we will update the effective date shown above. Material changes may be communicated through the app or another appropriate channel.
